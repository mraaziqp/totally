## 💡 **Common Questions & Answers**

### **Q: How long does it take to set up?**
A: About 30 minutes for the basics. You can take your time and enhance it over weeks.

### **Q: Can I make mistakes?**
A: Yes! And they're easy to fix. Just re-edit and save again. No permanent damage.

### **Q: Will customers see my changes immediately?**
A: Yes! Within 2-3 seconds of clicking "Save & Publish", changes are live.

### **Q: Can multiple people access my dashboard?**
A: Yes, but there's one shared password. Contact us if you want individual logins.

### **Q: What if I want to delete something?**
A: Just leave it blank and save. Or contact admin if you need help.

### **Q: Can I edit on my phone?**
A: Yes! Dashboard works on all devices.

### **Q: How often should I update content?**
A: Weekly is ideal. At minimum, monthly.

### **Q: Who manages the services/products list?**
A: Your admin (you handle the descriptions and content though).

### **Q: What if email testing fails?**
A: Check spam folder, wait 30 seconds, try again. Usually works within 2 tries.

### **Q: Can I upload images directly?**
A: Yes! Paste image URLs or reference uploaded files.

### **Q: Where do I get images if I don't have photos?**
A: Unsplash.com, Pexels.com, or Pixabay.com (all free). Or use your own!

**Common Issues & Solutions:**

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Can't login | Try caps lock OFF, clear cache, try different browser |
| Changes not showing | Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R), click save again, wait 5 sec |
| Image not loading | Check URL is correct, try different image |
| Email test fails | Wait 30 sec, check spam, try different email |
| Forgot password | Contact admin to reset |
