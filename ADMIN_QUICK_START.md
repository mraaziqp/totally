# 🚀 Admin Dashboard - Quick Start Guide

## Access Your Dashboard in 3 Steps

### **Step 1: Open the Admin URL**
Go to one of these URLs in your browser:

| Unit | URL |
|------|-----|
| **Deep Cleaning** | https://cleandeep.co.za/admin/deep-cleaning |
| **Pressure Cleaning** | https://cleandeep.co.za/admin/pressure-cleaning |
| **Gifting** | https://cleandeep.co.za/admin/gifting |

### **Step 2: Enter Your Password**
- A login screen will appear
- Enter your admin password
- Click "Unlock Dashboard"

### **Step 3: Choose a Tab**
Two main sections:
- **Lead Manager** - View incoming customer inquiries
- **Storefront Editor** - Edit all your content

## How to Add Images

### **Easy Method: Use a URL**
1. Get an image URL (from Unsplash, Imgur, or any hosting)
2. Paste the URL in the "Image URL" field
3. See instant preview
4. Click "Save & Publish"

### **Better Method: Upload Local Files**
1. Prepare your images (JPG or PNG)
2. Upload files to: `public/images/[your-unit]/`
   - For deep-cleaning: `public/images/deep-cleaning/photo1.jpg`
   - For pressure-cleaning: `public/images/pressure-cleaning/photo1.jpg`
   - For gifting: `public/images/gifting/photo1.jpg`
3. In the dashboard, reference as: `/images/[your-unit]/photo1.jpg`
4. Click "Save & Publish"

---

## Publishing Your Changes

1. **Make your edits** in the form
2. **Review** using the "View Live Storefront" link
3. **Click** "Save & Publish Storefront" button
4. **Wait** for confirmation message
5. **Your changes are live!** ✨

## Writing Tips

### **Headlines** 📝
- Keep under 60 characters
- Make them compelling
- Example: "Professional Deep Cleaning That Transforms Spaces"

### **Descriptions** 📝
- Be clear and specific
- Focus on customer benefits
- 2-3 sentences is ideal
- Avoid jargon

### **Testimonials** 📝
- Sound natural and authentic
- Include specific results
- Use real customer quotes
- 2-4 sentences

### **Images** 📸
- Use high-quality photos
- Show before/after when possible
- Include people (builds trust)
- First image appears larger

## Troubleshooting

### Image won't load?
- Check the URL starts with `https://` (not `http://`)
- Try a different image
- Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)

### Changes aren't appearing?
- Make sure you clicked "Save & Publish"
- Refresh the page
- Wait 30 seconds
- Clear browser cache

### Can't log in?
- Check password (case-sensitive)
- Make sure Caps Lock is off
- Ask your administrator for the password
