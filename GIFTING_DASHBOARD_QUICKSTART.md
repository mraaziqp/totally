## 🎯 **First 30 Minutes - Quick Start**

### **Minute 0-2: Login**
1. Go to https://cleandeep.co.za/admin/gifting
2. Enter password: `totally2026`
3. Click "Unlock Dashboard"

### **Minute 3-7: Check Your Orders**
1. Click **LEADS** tab
2. See all customer order requests
3. Note delivery dates
4. Plan delivery schedule

### **Minute 8-12: Test Email System**
1. Click **EMAIL** tab
2. Enter your email address
3. Click "Send Test Email"
4. Check your inbox
5. Confirm you got the test email ✅

### **Minute 13-18: Update Contact Info**
1. Click **PAGES** tab
2. Scroll down to "Hero & Branding" (Step 1)
3. Find "Contact Phone / WhatsApp" field
4. Update your phone number (for order queries)
5. Find "Contact Email" field
6. Enter your email (for order confirmations)
7. Find "Delivery / Availability Note" field
8. Example: "Free delivery within Panorama & Plattekloof area"

### **Minute 19-25: Add Your Brand Story**
1. Find "About & Values Section" (Step 3)
2. Enter your brand name in "Section Title"
3. Write a short story (2-3 sentences) about your gifting business
4. Example:
   ```
   "At Totally Gifting, we believe every occasion deserves 
   a special, thoughtful gift. We curate unique, beautiful 
   gifts for birthdays, anniversaries, and celebrations. 
   Each gift is wrapped with care and delivered with love."
   ```

### **Minute 26-30: Save Everything**
1. Scroll to bottom of page
2. Click pink button: **"Save & Publish Storefront"**
3. Wait for success message
4. Go to https://cleandeep.co.za/services/gifting
5. Refresh and see your changes live! 🎉

## 📸 **Adding Product Photos (CRITICAL FOR SALES!)**

### **What to Photograph**
- Gift packages (wrapped beautifully!)
- Individual gift items
- Gift boxes (nicely arranged)
- Special occasion gifts
- Premium/luxury options
- Bundle deals

### **Photography Tips**
- Use good lighting (natural light best)
- Include gift wrapping (shows presentation)
- Show close-ups of details
- Multiple angles of each product
- Lifestyle shots (person receiving gift)
- All arranged neatly

### **How to Add Photos**

1. Click **PAGES** tab
2. Scroll to "Gallery Images & Media" (Step 5)
3. Click **"Add Image"** button
4. Paste image URL
5. Add caption like: "Premium Rose & Chocolate Box"
6. Save & Publish

### **Gallery Organization**
- First image: Your BEST selling product
- Next 3-4: Popular gift options
- Next 3-4: Luxury items
- Last 2-3: Custom/special orders

## 🆘 **Quick Troubleshooting**

### **Can't Login?**
- Check caps lock is OFF
- Password is: `totally2026` (case sensitive)
- Clear browser cache & try again
- Try different browser

### **Changes Not Showing?**
- Click "Save & Publish" again
- Hard refresh browser: Ctrl+Shift+R
- Wait 5 seconds
- Try different browser

### **Image Not Loading?**
- Check image URL is complete
- Make sure it starts with https://
- Try a different image
- Contact support if still broken

### **Email Test Failed?**
- Wait 30 seconds & check inbox
- Check spam folder
- Try different email address
- Contact support if still failing
