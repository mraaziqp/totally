## 📧 Email Testing - How It Works

### **Step 1: Access Email Manager**
1. Go to your dashboard: https://cleandeep.co.za/admin/[unit]
2. Login with password
3. Click **Email Manager** tab

### **Step 2: Send Test Email**
1. Enter your email address
2. Click **Send Test Email**
3. Wait 1-2 seconds
4. Check your inbox

### **Step 3: Verify Delivery**
✅ **Test Passed** - Email arrived within seconds  
✅ **Service Working** - Resend is functioning properly  
✅ **Bookings Working** - Customers will receive confirmations

## 🖼️ Image Upload - How It Works

### **Step 1: Access Gallery**
1. Click **Image Gallery** tab
2. See upload area with icon

### **Step 2: Upload Images**
**Method 1:** Click the upload box
1. Click the dashed border area
2. Select images from your computer
3. Multiple images at once

**Method 2:** Drag & Drop
1. Drag images from your computer
2. Drop them on the upload area
3. Upload starts automatically

### **Step 3: Manage Images**
- See preview of uploaded images
- Hover to see delete option
- Delete unwanted images
- Reorder by uploading in order
