## Step 3️⃣: Click EMAIL Tab (2 minutes)

**Now:**
1. Click the **EMAIL** button/tab
2. You'll see email information
3. Below that is a box to enter your email

**What it looks like:**
```
Test Email Service

[Your email address here] [Send Test Email button]
```

**Now:**
1. Click in the email box
2. Type YOUR email address
3. Click "Send Test Email"

**WAIT:** 30 seconds

**Then:**
1. Check your inbox
2. Look for an email from "noreply@resend.dev"
3. You should have a test email! ✅

---

## Step 4️⃣: Click PAGES Tab (8 minutes)

**Now:**
1. Click the **PAGES** tab
2. You'll see a long form with sections

**What you see:**
```
Section 0: Page Settings
Section 1: Hero & Branding ← THIS ONE!
Section 2: Services Section
Section 3: About & Values
Section 4: Client Testimonial
Section 5: Gallery Images
```

**Find this field:**
Look for text that says: **"Contact Phone / WhatsApp"**

**Update your phone number:**
1. Click in the phone number field
2. Delete the old number (Ctrl+A then Delete)
3. Type YOUR phone number
4. Example: `072 359 1276`

✅ **Your phone number is now updated in the form!**

---

## Step 5️⃣: Save Your Changes (2 minutes)

**Now:**
1. **SCROLL ALL THE WAY DOWN** to bottom of page
2. Look for big **GREEN BUTTON**
3. It says: **"Save & Publish Storefront"**
4. Click it

**What happens:**
- Button shows "Publishing Updates..."
- Wait 3-5 seconds
- Button returns to normal

✅ **Your changes are now LIVE!**

---

## Step 6️⃣: Verify on Live Website (1 minute)

**Now:**
1. Open a NEW tab in your browser
2. Go to: `https://cleandeep.co.za/services/[yourunit]`
   - Replace `[yourunit]` with:
     - `deep-cleaning` OR
     - `pressure-cleaning` OR  
     - `gifting`
3. Press Enter
4. Look at the top of the page
5. Do you see YOUR phone number? ✅

**If yes:** Perfect! You're done with Day 1! 🎉

**If no:** 
- Wait 5 more seconds

- Check again
