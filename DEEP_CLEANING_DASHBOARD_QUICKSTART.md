## 🎯 **First 30 Minutes - Quick Start**

### **Minute 0-2: Login**
1. Go to https://cleandeep.co.za/admin/deep-cleaning
2. Enter password: `totally2026`
3. Click "Unlock Dashboard"

### **Minute 3-7: Check Your Leads**
1. Click **LEADS** tab
2. See all customer booking requests
3. Note any new leads
4. Plan follow-ups

### **Minute 8-12: Test Email System**
1. Click **EMAIL** tab
2. Enter your email address
3. Click "Send Test Email"
4. Check your inbox
5. Confirm you got the test email ✅

### **Minute 13-18: Update Contact Info**
1. Click **PAGES** tab
2. Scroll down to "Hero & Branding" (Step 1)
3. Find "Contact Phone / WhatsApp" field
4. Update your phone number
5. Find "Contact Email" field
6. Enter your email

### **Minute 19-25: Add Your Story**
1. Find "About & Values Section" (Step 3)
2. Enter your company name in "Section Title"
3. Write a short story (2-3 sentences) about your business
4. Example:
   ```
   "Deep Soft Cleaning has been serving our community 
   for 15 years with professional, eco-friendly cleaning 
   solutions. We believe every home deserves spotless care 
   with attention to detail."
   ```

### **Minute 26-30: Save Everything**
1. Scroll to bottom of page
2. Click green button: **"Save & Publish Storefront"**
3. Wait for success message
4. Go to https://cleandeep.co.za/services/deep-cleaning
5. Refresh and see your changes live! 🎉

## 🆘 **Quick Troubleshooting**

### **Can't Login?**
- Check caps lock is OFF
- Password is: `totally2026` (case sensitive)
- Clear browser cache & try again
- Try different browser

### **Changes Not Showing?**
- Click "Save & Publish" again
- Hard refresh browser: Ctrl+Shift+R
- Wait 5 seconds
- Try different browser

### **Image Not Loading?**
- Check image URL is complete
- Make sure it starts with https://
- Try a different image
- Contact support if still broken

### **Email Test Failed?**
- Wait 30 seconds & check inbox
- Check spam folder
- Try different email address
- Contact support if still failing
