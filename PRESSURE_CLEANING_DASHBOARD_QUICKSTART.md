## 📸 **Adding Before/After Photos (CRITICAL FOR YOUR BUSINESS!)**

### **Why Before/After Matters**
Pressure washing transformations are dramatic and convince customers instantly!

### **What to Photograph**
- Dirty driveway → Clean driveway
- Stained deck → Restored deck
- Dirty fence → Like-new fence
- Mouldy roof → Clean roof
- Grimy walls → Spotless walls

### **How to Add Photos**

1. Click **PAGES** tab
2. Scroll to "Gallery Images & Media" (Step 5)
3. Click **"Add Image"** button
4. Paste image URL from your phone/camera
5. Add caption like: "Driveway - Before & After"
6. Save & Publish

### **Best Practices**
- Take photos in good lighting
- Include the full area being cleaned
- Show dirt/stains clearly in "before" shot
- Show clean result in "after" shot
- Order them logically (before on left, after on right in description)

## 🆘 **Quick Troubleshooting**

### **Can't Login?**
- Check caps lock is OFF
- Password is: `totally2026` (case sensitive)
- Clear browser cache & try again
- Try different browser

### **Changes Not Showing?**
- Click "Save & Publish" again
- Hard refresh browser: Ctrl+Shift+R
- Wait 5 seconds
- Try different browser

### **Image Not Loading?**
- Check image URL is complete
- Make sure it starts with https://
- Try a different image
- Contact support if still broken

### **Email Test Failed?**
- Wait 30 seconds & check inbox
- Check spam folder
- Try different email address
- Contact support if still failing
